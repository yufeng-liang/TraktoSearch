    王漢宗中隸書繁 is a registered trademark of HtWang Graphics Laboratory

Two caveats worth knowing before this font is reused elsewhere:

1. The GPL has no font exception here. Distributing it inside an APK means
   the font binary itself is a GPL work being conveyed, so the licence text
   ships alongside it (this file) and the subsetted TTF keeps every original
   `name` record, including the copyright, trademark and licence notices.
2. The 王漢宗 series has a disputed provenance — justfont and others have
   documented 侵權爭議 over parts of the family. The release above is the one
   Dr. Wang put under the GPL, but the dispute is public and unresolved.

What ships in this repo:

app/src/main/res/font/hanwang_lisu_seal.ttf — 235 KB subset, 455 glyphs.
Only the Traditional Chinese seal keywords from assets/quotes.json
(`keyword["zh-Hant"]`) are kept; the full font is 8.1 MB. Produced with:

    python -m fontTools.subset HanWangLiSuMedium.ttf \
      --text-file=zh.txt --output-file=hanwang_lisu_seal.ttf \
      --layout-features= --no-hinting --drop-tables+=DSIG \
      --name-IDs='*' --recalc-bounds

`--name-IDs='*'` is deliberate: it keeps the copyright, trademark and GPL
records listed above inside the subsetted file.

Because the subset covers exactly those keywords, this font is only usable
for the Chinese seal. Adding a keyword to quotes.json means regenerating the
subset, or that keyword silently falls back to the system serif.
